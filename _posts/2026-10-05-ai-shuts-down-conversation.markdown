# AI shuts down conversations

When you and your colleagues work on a problem together, you share an understanding of what the problem is.  

You share an envolving understanding of what the solution is.  

You agree on the final solution.  

Everyone is on the same page.  

**But this can be short-circuited.**  

It can be short-circuited if someone uses AI to simply produce the "right" answer.  

AI can produce an _enormous_ document in a very short time that is:

1. Convincingly worded.
2. Difficult to refute.

AI totally shuts down critical thinking. It shuts down the conversation. I think I've seen this called "cognitive surrender" in other places.
