# Growing pains

If you're suffering and you don't know why, you may be growing.  

You may want to escape.  

That's a sign.  

_Maybe_ do not necessarily try to escape immediately.  

Stick it out if you can.  

Fulfill your obligations and responsibilities.  

See what happens, if anything.
